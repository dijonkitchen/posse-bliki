The best way to follow this bliki is to subscribe to its RSS feed in a
feed reader. A reader checks the sites you follow and shows you their new
posts in the order they were published.

I'd suggest [FeedFlow](https://www.feedflow.dev/). It's free, open
source, and runs on Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and Linux, but any feed
reader works. New to feeds? [About Feeds](https://aboutfeeds.com/) is a
short guide.

## Why a feed

- **Open web standards.** The feed is
  [RSS 2.0](https://www.rssboard.org/rss-specification), also offered as
  [JSON Feed](https://www.jsonfeed.org/version/1.1/), and the blogroll is
  [OPML](https://opml.org/spec2.opml). They're published, free formats
  that no company owns, so any reader can use them and none can lock
  you in.
- **No algorithm.** The feed is a plain file that lists my posts, newest
  first, the same for everyone. Your reader shows what the sites you
  picked published, in the order they published it. Nothing ranks,
  hides, boosts or rewrites a post, and nothing slips in an ad.
- **No tracking.** No account, no email address, no analytics, no
  tracking pixels and no third-party scripts, in the feed or on this
  site. Your reader fetches the feed the way a browser fetches a page;
  GitHub, which hosts the files, sees those requests as any web host
  would, and I see none of them.
- **Yours to keep.** Your subscriptions live in your reader, and you can
  export them as OPML to move to another one. The feed lives on my own
  domain, so its address stays the same if I change how the site is
  built or hosted.

## How to follow

1. Get a feed reader, such as FeedFlow.
2. Add this bliki: copy its feed address below and paste it into your
   reader.
3. Add the sites I read too: download the [blogroll](blogroll.md) as an
   OPML file, below, and import it in your reader's settings to follow
   every site on it in one step.
