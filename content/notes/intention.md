Moving and doing with intention is important. Otherwise, you’d never take a vacation.

That flexibility helps you and your relationships grow too.

Exploring how to hand-make cavatelli pasta with your kids with semolina flour that you’ve never used before is an enlightening experience.

Something I can never reproduce is the how often the first time I make things, they often go particularly well. There’s something about the wonder and newness of something you haven’t done before that brings excitement to the product itself. It’s like when people say they taste the love put into it. You can taste the enthusiasm!

It can be a lot of work to gather quality, foreign ingredients and make things yourself, but it’s an experience no one can take away or replace either.
