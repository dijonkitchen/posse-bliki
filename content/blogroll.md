Sites I read and keep coming back to. Most have a feed, so you can follow
them the same way you can follow [this one](colophon.md).

- [Martin Fowler](https://martinfowler.com/) ([feed](https://martinfowler.com/feed.atom)): software design, and where the word "bliki" comes from.
- [Dan Luu](https://danluu.com/) ([feed](https://danluu.com/atom.xml)): long, careful looks at how software and the tech industry really work.
- [Tom MacWright](https://macwright.com/) ([feed](https://macwright.com/rss.xml)): the web, maps and making things simpler.
- [Justin Duke](https://jmduke.com/) ([feed](https://jmduke.com/feed.xml)): running a small software business and shipping less.
- [David Heinemeier Hansson](https://world.hey.com/dhh/) ([feed](https://world.hey.com/dhh/feed.atom)): Rails, monoliths and calm companies, after Signal v. Noise.
- [A Smart Bear](https://longform.asmartbear.com/) ([feed](https://longform.asmartbear.com/index.xml)): Jason Cohen on startups and simple, lovable, complete products.
- [Coding Horror](https://blog.codinghorror.com/) ([feed](https://blog.codinghorror.com/rss/)): Jeff Atwood on programming and the people who do it.
- [Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com/articles.html) ([feed](http://www.aaronsw.com/2002/feeds/pgessays.rss)): essays on startups and how to think.
- [Farnam Street](https://fs.blog/) ([feed](https://fs.blog/feed/)): mental models and better decisions.
- [Naval](https://nav.al/) ([feed](https://nav.al/feed)): wealth, happiness and leverage, in short form.
- [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/) ([feed](https://www.lesswrong.com/feed.xml)): rationality and changing your mind.
- [xkcd](https://xkcd.com/) ([feed](https://xkcd.com/atom.xml)): a webcomic of romance, sarcasm, math and language.
