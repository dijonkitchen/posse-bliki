As the world shifts back to working closer together, I wonder if it’s a good thing long-term. Sometimes we reminisce about old times.

Loneliness is not a disease; we shouldn’t treat the symptoms of the lack of community.

Sometimes it’s just as simple as walking with a little extra weight on you to get more blood pumping and improving your baseline.

People are like this too. May not need therapy all the time, but it’s a good way to open a conversation.

It’s a wonderful world.
