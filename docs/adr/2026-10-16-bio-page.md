# 2026-10-16 — A bio page replaces LinkedIn

- Date: 2026-10-16
- Status: Proposed. Named "Bio" by JC on 2026-11-05.
- Raised by: JC, who asked for a professional bio on the site as a
  LinkedIn and résumé replacement, so people can find them for work.

## Context

JC's work history lived in a private résumé and on LinkedIn. Following
POSSE, the canonical copy should live on JC's own site, where it outlasts
any one platform and links to the writing behind it.

## Decision

- A page at `/bio/` (`content/bio.md`, aliases `/resume/` and `/cv/`)
  holds a short summary, what JC is looking for, how to get in touch,
  experience, skills and education. The intro, titles, dates, earlier finance career and
  education match JC's LinkedIn profile (JC asked for the summary to
  match, 2026-11-05); the experience details come from JC's own résumé.
  Nothing was invented.
- It is a summary, not a full résumé: it says a full résumé or a PDF
  copy is available by email, and JC sends those on request.
- It is called "Bio", since it isn't a full résumé. `/resume/` and `/cv/`
  redirect to it, for people who guess those addresses. "Work" wasn't
  available: the 2017 post `notes/work.md` owns that stem and `/work/`.
- It is a plain Markdown page.
- It is not in the header nav, which JC kept to three links so it doesn't
  wrap on phones (ADR 2026-11-05-now-in-the-header). Once it's published,
  the now page and the About page link to it.
- The visible name stays "JC", as on the rest of the site
  (ADR 2026-10-14-site-and-author-naming). Email uses the same riddle as
  About, "contact me at `${my-initials}` [at] MY_DOMAIN_NAME" (JC asked to
  stick with that wording, 2026-11-05), not a `mailto:` link, to keep
  scrapers off it.

## Consequences

- Recruiters and collaborators reach the work history from Now or About,
  and search engines index it on JC's domain.
- Updating the bio is editing one Markdown file. LinkedIn can link here.

## Alternatives considered

- **Extend the About page.** About is about the bliki; mixing in a work
  history buries both.
- **A header nav link.** One tap from every page, but a fourth link wraps
  on small phones in several styles.
- **An h-resume microformat.** The draft h-resume vocabulary would need raw
  HTML or a custom template, and no common tool consumes it. The home
  page's h-card already identifies JC.
- **Call it "Résumé".** The usual US word for a short work history, and
  what recruiters search for, but JC preferred a name that doesn't promise
  the full document.
- **"Work with me" at `/hire/`.** A call to action, but a less familiar
  address than `/bio/`.
- **A PDF résumé on the site.** Harder to read on a phone, not indexed as
  well, and drifts from the page; JC sends one on request instead.
