Fighting against the randomness of entropy is hard. Maybe one way is to schedule important things at awkward cadences. While not truly random, they can be forcing functions to redirect your attention.

I recently discovered apps for gathering people together with public events. It’s nice to easily schedule informal things and have them connect you with family, friends, and strangers. It doesn’t have to be just annual birthday parties.

Make every lemon crisis an opportunity for new lemonade.
